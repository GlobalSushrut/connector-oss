# Dynamic Intelligence Manifold (DIM)

**Status:** Architecture SoT (implementation in progress)  
**Purpose:** Self-maintaining augmented brain-space for persistent probabilistic intelligence — regulates the *condition* of cognition without authorizing world effects.

## Thesis

Connector already has consequence admission (NF³ / PATE / ActionBinding), an effect field (AAPI), a belief field (Knot), and authority (contracts / WorldGrant / HITL). The missing layer is a **persistent cognitive phase-space** in which intelligence stays coherent, adaptive, and controllable over long horizons.

DIM is that substrate. An agent is a **trajectory** through dynamic state `Z_t`, not `prompt + model + tools`.

## Two control systems

| System | Controls | Must not |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Intelligence-dynamics (DIM)** | Coherence, entropy, plasticity, recall, verification, wake, Θ | Mint or widen authority |
| **Consequence (NF³/PATE)** | Whether a proposal may affect the world | Be flipped by high K/Ψ/Θ |

Hard law: high coherence, confidence, utility, or empowerment **never** implies authority. DIM cannot convert NF³ Fail → Pass.
