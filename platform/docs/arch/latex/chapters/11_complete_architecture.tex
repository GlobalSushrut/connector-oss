= kernel core, green = execution engines, purple = CLS and logic, orange =
protocols, teal = developer surfaces, amber = control plane, grey =
infrastructure. Data flow moves top-to-bottom. Layer numbers are on the right
margin.

\section{The Sixteen Runtime Engines}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.45}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}clXl@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\#} & \textbf{Engine} & \textbf{Responsibility} & \textbf{Layer} \\
\midrule
E-1  & Boot Engine           & 12-stage node initialisation, systemd sd\_notify, DR recovery & L5 \\
E-2  & Auth Engine           & TOTP, HMAC, Ed25519 key verification, session tokens & L2 \\
E-3  & Billing Engine        & Stripe Checkout, webhook entitlement updates, tier enforcement & L2 \\
E-4  & Telemetry Engine      & OpenTelemetry, Prometheus metrics, usage tracking, audit sink & L2 \\
E-5  & Cognitive Engine      & 11-layer substrate: tension, possibility, commitment, plan, reflection & L5 \\
E-6  & SOE\,/\,Surface Engine     & 25-module surface system: CID receipts, role redaction, receipt chain & L5 \\
E-7  & CLS Compiler Engine   & 6-pass compilation: Parse$\to$Desugar$\to$Lower$\to$Verify$\to$Optimise$\to$Emit & L4 \\
E-8  & Contract VM Engine    & Solution Contract execution lifecycle, signed receipt production & L4 \\
E-9  & Logic Plane Engine    & ContractRegistry, Pipeline\,/\,Parallel\,/\,Saga\,/\,MapReduce composition & L4 \\
E-10 & Memory Engine         & MemPacket, CID content-addressing, namespace types, GC & L6 \\
E-11 & Policy\,/\,Predicate Engine & 11-layer enforcement chain evaluated per kernel operation & L6 \\
E-12 & Data Pipeline Engine  & Kafka-style asset$\to$knowledge ingestion, validation, tagging & L5 \\
E-13 & Multi-agent Engine    & Coordination patterns, consensus, delegation, A2A sessions & L5 \\
E-14 & Sandbox Engine        & Isolation levels, resource limits, capability attenuation per agent & L5 \\
E-15 & Lifecycle Engine      & Birth$\to$Run$\to$Pause$\to$Kill state machine with full event trace & L5 \\
E-16 & Distributed Cell Engine & Multi-node topology, mesh, discovery, cell-to-cell CNP routing & L5 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
