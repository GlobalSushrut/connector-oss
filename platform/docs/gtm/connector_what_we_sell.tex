\section{What we are selling}

A \textbf{self-hosted governed intelligence kernel}.

Customers keep LangGraph, Crew, vLLM, Ollama, OpenAI, MCP. Those products do what they do. Connector sits \textbf{beneath} them and does the job they skip:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Charter} an intelligence $I$ (identity $\mu$, not a Linux PID).
  \item \textbf{Admit} every effect $A$ (talk, tool, world, share) --- or refuse it.
  \item \textbf{Keep} the world model ($/m$, $/k$) that outlives one graph.
  \item \textbf{Stop} this $I$ (interrupt thought, kill-switch, compensating undo).
  \item \textbf{Prove} who did what (hash-chained traces; council floor with live $\mu$).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{SKU:} one node (\texttt{connector-platform} + \texttt{connectorctl}), licensed, self-hosted. Not a chatbot. Not a GPU farm. Not Honor/CosmOS. Not ``we are LangGraph.''

{\small\muted{Claim language we may use: governed intelligence kernel (V1 buyer Agent OS). We must not say: Mei et al.\ AIOS, LLM-as-CPU, court-green, SOC2 certified, Honor Agentic OS, infrastructure GPU OS.}}

\section{Why they need it}

Gartner (25 Jun 2025): \textbf{over 40\% of agentic projects canceled by end-2027} --- cost, unclear value, or inadequate risk controls. Gartner (26 May 2026): applying \textbf{uniform} governance across agents will fail; map each agent to Observe / Advise / Act-with-approval / Act-autonomous; L4 needs rollback, circuit breaker, named owner, and a \textbf{five-minute stop}.

The buyer already has agents that \textbf{act}: they spend, read data, call tools, change customer state. The framework built the workflow. It did not build:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item a stop that is this agent, not the whole box;
  \item a cost cap before the invoice;
  \item isolation so two agents do not share a brain;
  \item an identity the model cannot invent;
  \item a trail an auditor will sit with (not a PDF policy, not a Slack export).
\end{enumerate}

They need Connector because \textbf{the coordination layer is the remaining lock-in} once MCP made tools portable. Without it they cannot ship L4, cannot answer ``who did that,'' and join the 40\%.

\section{Who will buy}

\textbf{Three-question qualifier.} (1)~Is the AI taking actions, not only producing text? (2)~When it is wrong, can you say which intelligence did it? (3)~Have you had an incident, or can you see the one coming? Yes / no / yes --- that is a customer.

\textbf{Not first:} teams still in chatbot PoC; buyers who want Honor-style device UX; buyers who want us to become their GPU/vLLM farm.

\section{Why us}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{We are the operating layer, not a fourth framework.} LangGraph and Crew stay. Identity, WM, admit, and stop do not live in their graph.
  \item \textbf{CPU, GPU, and intelligence are three kinds.} We do not sell ``LLM as CPU.'' vLLM is a device URL. $I$ is chartered intelligence. That is why we can swap engines without rewriting the fleet.
  \item \textbf{Isolation is default, not a prompt.} A sharing contract (human+root) mints a pore. Council is the same rule: named owner $\mu$, not a shared crew RAM.
  \item \textbf{Evidence is fail-closed.} Court-readiness never auto-greens. Art.9 inventory is a living list, not a spreadsheet. We will not sell a green checkbox as a certificate.
  \item \textbf{It is already running.} The bake-off list is \texttt{buyer\_surface} in the claim JSON --- not a roadmap slide.
\end{itemize}

\section{What we do not sell}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A rewrite of the customer's LangGraph / Crew / vLLM stack.
  \item World rewind (undo is compensating).
  \item Certified SOC2 / ISO / court-green from this brief.
  \item Honor / CosmOS device OS. Infrastructure GPU OS.
  \item Live multi-node fabric as the default (product SoT is single-node until soak + flag).
\end{itemize}

\section{How they buy}

\textbf{Entry:} one live workflow where AI already acts and a miss is expensive. We put that workflow on three sockets (completion \texttt{/v1}, syscall, world grant). They keep their thinker. They get identity, admit, stop, budget, and a trail.

\textbf{Pilot shape:} 30 days, self-hosted node, one $I$ (then two, to prove isolation and council). Success = they can stop this $I$, see who did what, and show a crossing that was refused.

\textbf{Expansion:} more $I$, more grants, council, SCIM/OIDC, Art.9 inventory as packaging --- same kernel.
