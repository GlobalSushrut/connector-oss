\section{Selection criteria}
Every company cleared five bars: (1) \textbf{visible workflow pain} already live in production; (2) a \textbf{believable budget owner} for a paid pilot; (3) \textbf{bounded wedge} --- one workflow, one team; (4) \textbf{collaborative posture} not buried in procurement; (5) \textbf{North America only}. Startups without evident funding, budget ownership, or acute pain are excluded.

\section{Fit score}
An \textbf{estimated pilot-fit score} (not a measured conversion rate) reflects realistic likelihood of reaching a real pilot given pain, budget, access, and openness. Only companies \textbf{70+} appear. \textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{Green}} = recommended first-approach targets.
