\chapter{The Problem and the Solution}

\section{The shift: from chat to action}

AI is no longer only drafting text. Teams deploy agents that:

\begin{itemize}
  \item issue refunds and route support cases,
  \item approve or block transactions,
  \item update records and trigger downstream workflows,
  \item make recommendations that change customer experience or revenue.
\end{itemize}

When AI only generated copy, mistakes were annoying. When AI \textbf{acts}, mistakes become \textbf{operational}: revenue, trust, escalations, compliance, and internal confidence.

\section{What teams cannot answer today}

After an incident, most organizations struggle with three questions:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{What happened?}
  \item \textbf{Why did it happen?}
  \item \textbf{Can we prove it?}
\end{enumerate}

Examples:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Support cannot explain why the AI issued a refund.
  \item Fintech cannot reconstruct why a transaction was flagged.
  \item Internal automation teams cannot show why a workflow took a different path.
\end{itemize}

\begin{warnbox}{Human version of the pain}
When something goes wrong, teams cannot clearly explain or prove what the AI did.
\end{warnbox}

\section{Downstream effects}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Automation slows down; humans pulled into firefighting.
  \item Buyers and customers lose trust.
  \item Security and compliance become blockers.
  \item AI initiatives lose momentum or stall in review.
\end{itemize}

\section{Why frameworks alone do not solve it}

Agent \textbf{frameworks} (orchestration libraries, LLM SDKs) help developers chain calls. They typically leave \textbf{governance, audit-grade evidence, cost enforcement, and recovery} to application code. In regulated or high-stakes environments that is insufficient:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Log lines are not legal-grade evidence.
  \item Post-hoc guardrails do not stop a tool call already in flight.
  \item Token spend is invisible until the invoice arrives.
  \item Compliance cannot be credibly bolted on after production launch.
\end{itemize}

\section{The Connector solution}

Connector solves this at the \textbf{infrastructure boundary}---the place where agents commit state, spend money, call tools, and affect customers.

\begin{conceptbox}{Solution in buyer language}
Connector is the \textbf{execution control layer} for AI decisions: trace every step, enforce policy at runtime, export proof your stakeholders can review.
\end{conceptbox}
