\section{GTM motion (2026)}

\subsection{Motion type}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Founder-led} direct outreach.
  \item \textbf{Network-based} warm intros.
  \item \textbf{Not} broad paid acquisition or ``platform tour'' webinars yet.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Four steps}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Identify} teams with live AI actions + recent or expected incident.
  \item \textbf{Sell one workflow} --- one pain, one pilot (2--6 weeks).
  \item \textbf{Land evidence} --- customer quote, export sample, before/after trace.
  \item \textbf{Expand} --- more workflows, more plugins, reference story.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Outreach framing that works}

\begin{insightbox}{Opening email / LinkedIn DM}
Are you using AI to take actions in support or ops?

When it makes the wrong call, can you explain why?

If not, we should talk.
\end{insightbox}

\subsection{2026 targets (honest)}

\textbf{By end of Q2:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 3--5 qualified conversations in wedge segments.
  \item 1--2 paid pilots in progress or completed.
  \item At least one documented pilot win with customer statement.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{By end of Q3:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 2--3 completed pilots with outcomes.
  \item At least 1 expansion conversation (more workflows or teams).
  \item Messaging refined from real objections.
\end{itemize}

\section{Pilot structure (reminder)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Scope:} one workflow, one team, defined success criteria.
  \item \textbf{Deliverables:} full trace, decision explanation, audit-ready export.
  \item \textbf{Success reaction:} ``Now we can see what happened.''
  \item \textbf{Not:} free indefinite POC of entire platform.
\end{itemize}

\section{Why they pay (buying triggers)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Unexplained AI decisions slowing operations.
  \item Customer escalations they cannot defend.
  \item Deals blocked by prospect security questions.
  \item Compliance review approaching without artifacts.
  \item Internal teams afraid to ship more AI without control.
\end{itemize}
