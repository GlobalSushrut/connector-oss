\chapter{Custom Workflows vs.\ Pre-Made Plugins}

This is one of the most common semi-technical questions: \emph{``Should we build our own workflow or turn on WitnessCtl / TraceTramp / DevGuard?''}

\section{Definitions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Pre-made (first-party) plugins} --- Maintained Connector applications (DevGuard, TraceTramp, WitnessCtl, LedgerLens, and planned Conductor, AgentLoop, etc.). Installed on the node; solve a \textbf{horizontal} problem (safety, trace, audit, cost).
  \item \textbf{Custom workflows (CLS)} --- Customer-specific automation: your refund logic, your risk rules, your internal routing. Authored for \textbf{your} business process; still runs \textbf{on} the Connector kernel so governance applies.
  \item \textbf{Community / AGOS plugins} --- Packaged \texttt{.cpkg} integrations (Slack, Jira, Datadog patterns). Faster than bespoke code for standard connectors.
\end{itemize}

\section{Why not only custom workflows?}

Building everything yourself recreates what the plugins already productize:

\begin{itemize}
  \item A proxy with metering, quarantine, and policy versioning (TraceTramp).
  \item Tamper-evident session export and compliance shells (WitnessCtl).
  \item Host-level enforcement for coding agents (DevGuard).
  \item CFO-grade attribution and budget gates (LedgerLens).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{GTM rule:} sell custom workflow \textbf{for the business story}; sell plugins \textbf{for the control plane}. Almost every serious deployment uses \textbf{both}.

\section{Why not only pre-made plugins?}

Plugins do not know your company's refund rules, credit policy, or internal escalation matrix. Without a custom workflow (or heavy configuration), you only get \textbf{infrastructure}, not \textbf{your} automated decision.

\begin{insightbox}{Combined pitch}
Pre-made plugins answer: \emph{``How do we govern AI in production?''}

Custom workflows answer: \emph{``How do we automate \textbf{our} decision safely?''}
\end{insightbox}

\section{Typical adoption path}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Week 1--2:} Node up + one plugin matching acute pain (often WitnessCtl or TraceTramp).
  \item \textbf{Week 2--4:} Wire \textbf{one} custom workflow through that boundary.
  \item \textbf{Month 2+:} Add plugins (LedgerLens for cost, DevGuard for eng agents).
  \item \textbf{Quarter 2+:} More workflows on catalog; platform team standardizes patterns.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What to say in a meeting}

\begin{quote}
\emph{``We are not asking you to throw away your agent. We give you a governed runtime boundary---TraceTramp or WitnessCtl---and your workflow runs inside it. You keep your business logic; we make it provable.''}
\end{quote}
