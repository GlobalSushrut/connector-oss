What audit needs

Auditability means you can trace a workflow from trigger to outcome, not just inspect a single final message.

What breaks without it

When something goes wrong, teams cannot explain tool usage, decision paths, or whether policy was followed.

What good evidence looks like

Reviewable traces, policy decisions, approval events, and tamper-evident receipts tied to agent actions.

AI agent audit trail requirements

  • Action-by-action traceability across a workflow
  • Decision explanations and policy context
  • Recorded approval and override history
  • Immutable or tamper-evident evidence chains
  • Searchable records for review, compliance, and incident response

Who uses AI agent audit trails

Security teams

They need proof of what an agent touched, changed, or attempted.

Compliance teams

They need evidence that policies and approvals were enforced consistently.

Operations leaders

They need a reliable answer when a workflow misfires and the business asks why.
