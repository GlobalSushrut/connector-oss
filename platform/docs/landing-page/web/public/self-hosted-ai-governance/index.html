Why self-hosted AI governance matters

Data sovereignty

Every policy check, every audit receipt, every agent decision stays within your network boundary. No telemetry, no routing of governed data to external services.

Compliance requirements

HIPAA, FedRAMP, IL4/IL5, and financial regulations often require that audit infrastructure itself be within your control boundary — not a third-party SaaS.

Restricted-network posture

Restricted-network / offline posture is a design-partner path — not a sold air-gap SKU. Governance still needs a model path if you use a vendor LLM.

Audit independence

When a regulator asks for your audit trail, it should come from infrastructure you control — not from a vendor who can modify retention policies or access your data.

What self-hosted AI governance actually requires

  • Single-binary deployment — no complex cloud dependencies to manage
  • Docker and Kubernetes compatible — runs in your existing container infrastructure
  • Offline policy evaluation — CCL contracts execute locally, no external calls
  • Local HMAC-chained receipts on your hardware — issuer HMAC, not court-grade quorum
  • Self-hosted binary you operate — not marketed as a certified air-gap product
  • Self-contained audit storage — receipts and journals stored in your database
