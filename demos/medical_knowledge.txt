SECTION 2: CLINICAL GUIDELINES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

CHEST PAIN — ACC/AHA 2021
- ECG within 10 min, serial troponin 0h/1h or 0h/3h
- HEART Score: History(0-2), ECG(0-2), Age(0-2), Risk(0-2), Troponin(0-2)
  0-3=Low risk/discharge, 4-6=Moderate/observe, 7-10=High/admit+cath
- DDx: ACS, PE, dissection, pneumothorax, pericarditis, GERD, MSK
- Red flags: tearing to back (dissection), leg swelling (PE), JVD+hypotension (tamponade)

DIABETES — ADA 2024
- Dx: FPG≥126, A1C≥6.5%, 2hPG≥200, random PG≥200+symptoms
- A1C target: <7% most, <8% elderly, <6.5% new onset
- First-line: metformin 500mg BID→1000mg BID
- ASCVD/CKD: add SGLT2i regardless of A1C
- HF: SGLT2i preferred (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin)

HYPERTENSION — AHA/ACC 2017
- Stage 1: 130-139/80-89, Stage 2: ≥140/90
- First-line: ACEi/ARB, CCB, thiazide
- CKD→ACEi/ARB, HF→ACEi/ARB+BB, DM→ACEi/ARB
- Target: <130/80 most adults

PNEUMONIA — IDSA/ATS 2019
- CURB-65: Confusion, Urea>7, RR≥30, BP<90/60, Age≥65
  0-1=outpatient, 2=short stay, 3-5=ICU
- Outpatient: amoxicillin 1g TID or doxycycline 100mg BID
- Inpatient: ceftriaxone 1g IV + azithromycin 500mg
- ICU: ceftriaxone + azithromycin; MRSA risk→add vancomycin

ANTICOAGULATION — CHEST 2021
- DVT/PE: DOACs preferred (rivaroxaban 15mg BIDx21d→20mg daily)
- CHA2DS2-VASc for AF: ≥2(men)/≥3(women)→anticoagulate
- Duration: provoked 3mo, unprovoked ≥3mo (indefinite if low bleed risk)

SECTION 3: DRUG INTERACTIONS (CRITICAL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Warfarin + NSAIDs → GI hemorrhage
ACEi + K-sparing diuretics → Hyperkalemia (K+>5.5)
Metformin + IV contrast → Lactic acidosis (hold 48h)
SSRIs + MAOIs → Serotonin syndrome
Statins + macrolides → Rhabdomyolysis
Fluoroquinolones + QTc drugs → Torsades de pointes
Clopidogrel + omeprazole → Reduced antiplatelet effect
Lithium + NSAIDs → Lithium toxicity (range 0.6-1.2)
Digoxin + amiodarone → Digoxin toxicity (reduce 50%)
Methotrexate + trimethoprim → Pancytopenia

High-Alert (ISMP): anticoagulants, insulins, opioids, NMBAs, chemo, concentrated electrolytes

AST 10-40, ALT 7-56, ALP 44-147
Bilirubin 0.1-1.2, Albumin 3.5-5.0
INR 0.8-1.1 (warfarin therapeutic: 2.0-3.0)

Thyroid: TSH 0.4-4.0, fT4 0.8-1.8
Inflammatory: CRP <1.0, Procalcitonin <0.1=unlikely bacterial, >0.5=likely

SECTION 6: RADIOLOGY CRITERIA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Lung Nodule — Fleischner 2017:
Solid <6mm: no follow-up, 6-8mm: CT 6-12mo, >8mm: CT 3mo/PET/biopsy
GGO ≥6mm: CT 6-12mo then q2yr x5yr
Part-solid ≥6mm: CT 3-6mo, stable→annual x5yr

Lung-RADS: 1=negative, 2=benign, 3=probably benign, 4A=suspicious, 4B=very suspicious
BI-RADS: 0=incomplete, 1=neg, 2=benign, 3=prob benign, 4=suspicious, 5=highly suggestive, 6=known
